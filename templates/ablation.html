HOW TO READ THE TABLE
k
Top-k — the variable being ablated
How many chunks are retrieved and passed to the LLM. Higher k = more coverage (Hit↑) but more irrelevant chunks in the prompt (Precision↓, latency↑).
H
Hit@k — did we find the answer?
Primary metric. 1 if the correct source appears anywhere in the top-k results. This is what matters most — if the correct chunk is missing, the LLM cannot answer correctly.
P
Precision@k — quality of retrieval
What fraction of the k chunks are from the right source? Low precision means the prompt is full of noise. High k + low precision = longer prompt for no benefit.
S
Strategy — fixed vs paragraph
fixed: 400-char sliding window — consistent length, easier for the embedder. paragraph: natural boundaries — better semantic coherence but variable length.
